# Foot lock: generation-11 fine-tunes (2026-11-01)

Native foot contact for InstantHMR, measured before the final evaluation. All
models are B4 at 288 px. g11 arms are 120-epoch fine-tunes of g10m75_b4
(epoch 474), LR 1e-4.

| arm | previous-pose token | semelle data | notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| g10m75_b4 | no | no | baseline |
| g11Ap_b4 | yes, `--prev-pairs-only --prev-mask-p 0.2` | yes, 4 participants, `--w-foot-mocap 0.35` | the paper model |
| g11Cp_b4 | yes, same flags | no (contact from Harmony4D motion only) | token-only ablation |
| g11A_b4 / g11C_b4 | yes, old recipe (stills fed their own GT as the previous pose) | A yes / C no | superseded; lag on video |

Checkpoint: **`last.pth` (epoch 119)**. `best_student_model_v3.pth` is chosen on
3DPW validation and lands at epoch 3-6 (the initialisation), blind to foot lock.

## Protocol

- **Foot lock** (`benchmark/eval_footlock.py`): 6 held-out sessions of 4
  participants never seen in training, same room and cameras as training,
  156 trial-cameras, 166,663 foot pairs in insole contact. Frames every 5 video
  frames (125 ms at 40 fps), undistorted, calibrated focal.
  - *slide*: mean displacement of a foot (mean of its 4 keypoints, camera
    frame) between consecutive labelled frames while the insole says it is in
    contact in both. Mocap feet on the same pairs: 1.83 mm.
  - *foot error*: foot keypoints vs mocap markers after removing each trial's
    median offset.
  - *contact F1*: per-foot contact head (logit > 0) vs insole contact.
  - *masked*: previous pose withheld. *feedback*: the model's own prediction on
    the previous labelled frame (125 ms earlier).
- **3DPW test**: every frame, GT-joint boxes, J14 PA-MPJPE with the adapter,
  H36M GT. **EMDB-1**: SMPL24 PA-MPJPE with the adapter. *feedback* = the
  model's own prediction 5 frames earlier in the same track.
- *jitter*: mean second difference of pelvis-centred joints over 3 consecutive
  frames (mm/frame^2).

## Post-hoc foot lock (`eval_footlock.py --lock`)

While a foot is called planted, its keypoints are held at the running average of
its predicted position since contact onset (causal); released, the raw
prediction returns. *lock* = Ap's own contact head, *oracle* = insole contact.
Slide is near zero by construction; foot error is the test. *Release jump* is
the foot's displacement on the release pair and includes genuine lift-off
motion (not yet compared with the raw prediction on the same pairs).

| model | variant | slide mm | foot err mm | release jump mm |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| g11Ap ep119 feedback | none | 11.07 | 30.41 | - |
| g11Ap ep119 feedback | lock | 1.73 | 27.26 | 126.9 |
| g11Ap ep119 feedback | oracle | 1.63 | 25.58 | 98.0 |
| g11Ap ep119 masked | lock / oracle | 1.81 / 1.70 | 26.52 / 25.05 | 134.1 / 105.1 |
| g10m75_b4 | oracle | 2.75 | 35.62 | 118.3 |

## Contact detection on jumps (held-out, g11Ap ep119)

22 jump trial-cameras; frames where the insoles give a valid label for both feet.

| insole says | frames | model: 0 planted | 1 planted | 2 planted |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| both feet off (flight) | 500 | 83.2% | 15.0% | 1.8% |
| one foot off | 274 | 3.3% | 68.2% | 28.5% |
| both feet on | 10,191 | 0.3% | 0.7% | 99.0% |

Feedback; masked is within 2.5 points. On flight frames where exactly one foot
stays "planted", it is the left foot 70 times and the right 5 times.

## Demo path

`demo.py` with `models/instanthmr_g11Ap_b4_ep119.onnx` (own RF-DETR boxes, no
undistortion, previous pose fed back from 5 frames earlier), one held-out
walking trial, 532 insole-labelled frames: 93.5% agreement, F1 0.956 (withheld:
93.0%, 0.953). 43 fps end to end on an RTX 4070 (InstantHMR 7.0 ms).
